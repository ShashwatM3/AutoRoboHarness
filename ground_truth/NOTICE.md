## What we use

A small subset (21 of the 42,000 per-camera clips): 14 `take`, 4 `put` and 3 `touch` clips. Each clip is a single camera view of a single annotated action. `library.json` lists every clip with its original `filepath` in the dataset.

## Modifications (derivative works)

- `clips/*.mp4`: original clip files, unmodified, renamed to `<clip_id>.mp4` (for example `data/data_6/rank0_video-3443.mp4` becomes `clips/d6_rank0_3443.mp4`).
- `frames/<clip_id>/f1.jpg`–`f4.jpg`: 4 frames extracted with ffmpeg at evenly spaced indices inside the annotated action's frame range, downscaled from 600×600 to 480×480 and JPEG re-encoded.
- `frames/<clip_id>/sheet.jpg`: a 2×2 contact sheet of those 4 frames, with a red square drawn at the dataset's annotated hand-contact point (`interaction_x`, `interaction_y`).
- `library.json`: the dataset's labels (action class, frame range, interaction point, split, camera view) plus text descriptions and attributes (`description`, `hands`, `shelf_height`, `looked_or_touched_first`, `item_size`). The text was generated by `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL` (served by Crusoe) from the contact sheets. It was not written or checked by Standard AI.

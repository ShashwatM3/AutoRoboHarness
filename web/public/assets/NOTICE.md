# Store assets

Kenney's Mini Market and Food Kit GLB models were downloaded from the official asset pages during the event. Both packs are licensed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. Original license files are included in each pack directory.

- Mini Market: https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-market
- Food Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit
- CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The store engine, scene assembly, robot model and animation code are original project contributions. The third-party packs supply store furnishings and food meshes.

# Store assets

Kenney's Mini Market, Food Kit, and Mini Characters GLB models were downloaded from the official asset pages during the event. All three packs are licensed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. Original license files are included in each pack directory.

- Mini Market: https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-market
- Food Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit
- Mini Characters: https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters
- CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The store engine, scene assembly, robot model and animation code are original project contributions. The third-party packs supply store furnishings, food meshes, and animated shopper characters.
